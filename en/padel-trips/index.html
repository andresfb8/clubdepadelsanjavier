The idea

A holiday with a racket

You pick the dates and tell us your level. We set up your training, get you into matches with club players and point you to the best spots nearby.

  • Small-group sessions or private lessons
  • Matches and mini-tournaments with club members
  • For couples, groups of friends or teams
  • All levels, including complete beginners
  • We'll look after you in English or Spanish
  • We'll help you find somewhere to stay nearby
To give you an idea

A typical day

Morning

Session with your coach

Technique, tactics and plenty of balls. We work on whatever you most want to improve.

Midday

Mar Menor

A swim, the beach, rice by the sea or a wander along La Manga. Your call.

Evening

Match at the club

Put the morning's work into practice against players at your level. Then see where the evening goes.

This is just an example: every trip is built around your dates, your level and how much you want to play.

The place

Why San Javier?

A laid-back town on the shore of the Mar Menor in south-east Spain, with good weather most of the year and a club that treats you as one of its own from day one.

Sunshine, mostly

We play outdoors here in winter too.

Sea on the doorstep

The Mar Menor and its beaches are minutes from the court.

Easy to reach

Murcia and Alicante airports are both within reach.

A real club

No tourist bubble: you play with the people who live here.
