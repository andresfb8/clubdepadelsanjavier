The place

Why San Javier?

San Javier sits on the shore of the Mar Menor, on the Costa Cálida in south-east Spain. Its dry Mediterranean climate, with mild winters and very few rainy days, makes outdoor training possible twelve months a year.

2,621 hours of sunshine a year

More than seven hours of sun a day on average, and over 150 hours even in December.

17.6 °C annual average

Daytime highs of around 16 °C in January and 29.5 °C in August, tempered by the sea breeze.

33 rainy days a year

Just 313 mm of rainfall annually. From June to August it hardly rains at all.

Two airports nearby

Región de Murcia (RMU) is about 30 minutes by car and Alicante-Elche (ALC) under an hour.

Climate data: AEMET (Spanish Met Office), San Javier Airport station, 1981-2010 normals.
