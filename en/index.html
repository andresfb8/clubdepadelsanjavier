FAQ

Before you come

The questions we get most on WhatsApp, already answered.

Can I join if I've never played?

Of course. There are beginner groups in both the junior academy and the adult classes, and every group is set by level, so you start alongside people at the same stage as you.

How much do classes cost?

The junior academy starts at €20 a month. Adult classes are €30 to €45 a month. Private lessons start at €30 an hour, plus €5 for each extra player.

How often do groups train, and how big are they?

Academy groups train twice a week. Adults can choose mornings or evenings, with a maximum of four players per court.

I don't have a partner to compete with. Can I still join?

Yes, we'll find you a match. There are in-house leagues by category and Friday mini-tournaments, which are a great way to meet the rest of the club.

I'm only here on holiday for a few days. Can I train?

Yes. You can book private lessons at flexible times, or ask us for a tailor-made padel trip with coaching and matches with club members.

Do you speak English?

Yes, we'll look after you in English or Spanish.

Where do you train?

At the San Javier sports centre (Polideportivo) and at Polígono Industrial El Pino (Av. de la Unión), both in San Javier, Murcia.

How do I sign up?

Message us on WhatsApp with your level and availability. We reply within the hour and find you a group.
